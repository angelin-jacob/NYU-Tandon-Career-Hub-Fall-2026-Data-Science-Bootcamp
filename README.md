## How each session works

1. **Lecture.** Concepts and examples, taught with slides and Jupyter notebooks.
2. **Hands-on exercises.** Apply the concepts right away with real datasets and Python.
3. **Q&A.** Ask questions at any time. The more you ask, the more you learn.
4. **Take-home assignment.** Problems on the week's topic.
5. **Breaks.** Several short breaks during each two-hour session.

## Weekly assignments

- Assignments are on **Brightspace** ([Section 1](https://brightspace.nyu.edu/d2l/home/561525) · [Section 2](https://brightspace.nyu.edu/d2l/home/639262)). Submit your notebook as a file through **Brightspace › Assignments**.
- Each assignment is **due one week after it is assigned**, by **11:59 PM** on your section's day (Tuesday or Thursday).
- You may work with classmates, but **every student submits their own work**.
- If you miss a class, the assignment is still due on time. If you need more time, email datasciencebootcamp@nyu.edu **before** the deadline.

### Naming your submission

Name every notebook **`netid_week_number.ipynb`**, in lowercase with no spaces. For example, NetID `ab1234` submits Week 1 as `ab1234_week_1.ipynb`.

Make the first cell a Markdown cell with your details:

```markdown
# Week 1 Take-Home
**Name:** First Last
**NetID:** ab1234
**Section:** 1 (Tuesday) or 2 (Thursday)
```

Put each answer under a heading that matches the exercise, for example `## Exercise 1: Vowel counter`. If you skip an exercise, keep its heading and write *Not attempted* under it.

## Final project

The project is **required**. Groups of **3–4 students** work on a real-world problem and present to industry professionals, once mid-program for feedback and once at the end. **Prizes go to the winning group.**

### Project options (choose one)

| Project | Dataset |
|---|---|
| Data Visualization with Air Quality Data | [Air Quality in India](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shrutibhargava94/india-air-quality-data/data) |
| Sentiment Analysis of Social Media Content | [Social Media Sentiments](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kashishparmar02/social-media-sentiments-analysis-dataset/code) |
| Movie Recommendation System | [TMDB Movie Metadata](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/tmdb/tmdb-movie-metadata) |
| Finance & Economics Time-Series Analysis | [Finance & Economics Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/khushikyad001/finance-and-economics-dataset-2000-present/data) |

The tasks and timelines in the syllabus are suggestions. To pursue your own idea instead, email your instructor or datasciencebootcamp@nyu.edu for approval.

### Key dates

| Date | Milestone |
|---|---|
| Mon, Oct 5 | Bootcamp social: meet classmates and form groups |
| Sun, Oct 18, 11:59 PM | Group names and chosen prompt due |

### Judging criteria

Each criterion is scored from 1 (lowest) to 3 (highest).

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Project Design:** was it planned well, and did the group ask questions that moved it forward? | Poorly designed; no tangible results | Moves forward, but the approach is disjointed | Moves forward in a systematic way |
| **Addressing the Stated Problem:** how well do the results solve it? | Does not address the problem | Missing details; inconclusive results | Solves the stated problem |
| **Presentation & Demo:** how clear was it? | Lacks depth and clarity | Little detail; hard to follow | Clear, concise, easy to follow |
